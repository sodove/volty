Two styles, one palette

Toggle between Clean (Material 3 Expressive cards) and Classic VESC — the overlapping dial cluster of the RT-DATA screen, but bezels, ticks, needles and arcs all take Material You colors instead of the original grey/white.

The inner gauge is the rider's choice — per vehicle

A wheel needs duty/ШИМ in the middle; a scooter wants battery; a bike maybe power or motor temp. Secondary gauge is a per-vehicle setting — pick once, it sticks.
