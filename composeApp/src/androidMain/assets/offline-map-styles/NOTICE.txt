Bundled OpenFreeMap style snapshots
===================================

This directory contains the dark and bright OpenFreeMap styles, sourced from
https://github.com/hyperknot/openfreemap-styles at commit
72e1480dfc92858d334647037988bd2591fdb021.  The original repository is
MIT-licensed: Copyright (c) 2023 Zsolt Ero.

Lineage of the two styles (they are not the same family):
- Bright derives from OpenMapTiles / OSM Bright GL Style
  (https://github.com/openmaptiles/osm-bright-gl-style).
- Dark derives from OpenMapTiles / Dark Matter GL Style
  (https://github.com/openmaptiles/dark-matter-gl-style), whose design is
  derived from CartoDB Basemaps by Stamen and Paul Norman for CartoDB Inc.,
  licensed CC BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
The code of these styles is BSD 3-Clause and their visual design CC BY 4.0
(CC BY 3.0 for the CartoDB-derived design of Dark).

CHANGES made by Volty (indications of modification, as CC BY requires):
- At runtime the snapshots are transformed to replace remote tile and font
  URLs with Volty's local PMTiles loopback server and to remove decorations
  that need an unavailable sprite atlas.
- The colours of selected layers are modified at runtime to follow the app's
  theme accent and light/dark mode: the background, land use, buildings and
  place and road labels in both styles, and in the dark style also the roads,
  road casings and footpaths.  Only paint colours are changed, by shifting the
  tone of each upstream colour; hues and chroma of those layers are replaced by
  a neutral tint of the chosen accent.  Water, parks, railways, boundaries and
  all geometry, sources, tile URLs, sprite and glyph references are untouched.
  The bright style keeps OpenFreeMap's own road colours.
- The dark style's labels are brighter than upstream for legibility.

Volty exposes the required map attribution in the map corner, as links:
(c) OpenFreeMap (c) OpenMapTiles (c) OpenStreetMap.  The attribution is also
shown on every route preview snapshot.
